# The whole course in five minutes

1. Point the agent at a messy folder.
2. Ask what it sees.
3. Ask for a plan.
4. Argue with the plan.
5. Let it work.
6. Inspect the result.

Today, we do exactly this.

# Four house rules

1. **Start with copies.**
2. **Ask for a plan before a large change.**
3. **Do not paste secrets or sensitive material.**
4. **Open the result and look at it.**

The model supplies speed. You remain responsible for the work.

---

# Talk like a colleague

You do not need incantations.

> Look through this folder. Tell me what you think it contains, what seems
> duplicated or misplaced, and what you would change. Do not change anything
> yet.

Then disagree. Add context. Change your mind. Ask it to show you.
