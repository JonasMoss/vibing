# This is not a course about commands.

It is a course about what becomes possible when an AI can work with your files,
use tools, and keep going after the first answer.

We will learn just enough terminal to get an agent into the right folder. Then
we will give it real jobs: cleaning a mess, checking research material, and
building something for a course. You do not need to become a programmer. You do
need to stay curious, make decisions, and inspect the result.

> The goal is one genuine **“that just saved me an afternoon”** moment for
> everyone in the room.
