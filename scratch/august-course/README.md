# The August 2026 course: postponed, never taught

The original three-day course. It was postponed on 11 August 2026, the day
before it was due to start, because of sick leave. The department promised the
registered participants new dates; it is coming back as the January 2027 course
(`courses/2027-01-faculty/`).

## Facts as planned (from the official invite, `AITerminal.pdf`)

- **Dates:** Wed 12 – Fri 14 August 2026. Three days, 10:00–16:00, lunch
  12:00–13:00. 15 hours in total. (`plan-2026-07.md` says 4 days; that is
  older, and the PDF wins.)
- **Room:** C2-040 "Harvard", Campus Oslo, an executive/MBA horseshoe room.
- **Capacity:** 30 places; about 50 applied, 28 confirmed by 7 August.

## Files

- `schedule.md`: the one-page timetable.
- `schedule-detailed.md`: block-by-block plan with instructor notes, the time
  budget, cut lists and the materials TODO list. Paths in it use the old
  layout; see `../README.md`.
- `in-the-room.md`: the page to hold while teaching: opening script,
  pre-decided branches, a floor and first cut for each block.
- `grievance-answers.md`: prepared answers to the usual objections (Day 3).
- `days/`: the participant-facing day pages from the old site.
- `plan-2026-07.md`: the very first plan, from July.
