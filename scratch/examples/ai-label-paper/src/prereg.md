**1. Have any data been collected for this study already?**
No, no data have been collected for this study yet.

**2. What's the main question being asked or hypothesis being tested?**
H1: A label saying that a product description was written by AI lowers purchase
intention. H2: This effect is stronger for a hedonic product (a scented candle)
than for a utilitarian product (a phone charger). H3: For the hedonic product,
perceived authenticity mediates the effect.

**3. Describe the key dependent variable(s) specifying how they will be measured.**
Primary: purchase intention, the mean of three 7-point items. Secondary:
willingness to pay, open-ended, in US dollars. Mediator: perceived authenticity,
the mean of four 7-point items; item 3 ("The description feels artificial") is
reverse-scored.

**4. How many and which conditions will participants be assigned to?**
Four, between subjects: 2 (label: "This description was written by AI" vs. no
label) × 2 (product: scented candle vs. phone charger).

**5. Specify exactly which analyses you will conduct to examine the main question/hypothesis.**
A 2 × 2 ANOVA on purchase intention. H1 is the main effect of the label; H2 is
the label × product interaction. H3: a mediation model for the candle (PROCESS
Model 4, 5,000 bootstrap samples).

**6. Describe exactly how outliers will be defined and handled, and your precise rule(s) for excluding observations.**
We will exclude participants who fail the attention check (an instructed-response
item). There will be no other exclusions.

**7. How many observations will be collected or what will determine sample size?**
640 US residents on Prolific, 160 per condition.

**8. Anything else you would like to pre-register?**
The manipulation check comes after the dependent variables, so that it cannot
influence them.
