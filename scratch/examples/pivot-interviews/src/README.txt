How founders decide to change direction early

Fictional practice material for a hands-on course on qualitative research with AI agents. Everything in this folder is fictional, including the interviews, people, and start-ups; it is not research evidence.

The study comprises eight semi-structured interviews with founders of young Norwegian start-ups, conducted in February 2026 and transcribed in English. Founders are labelled F01–F08. The transcripts were pseudonymised by the researcher, and firm names are replaced with [FIRM].

interview-guide.md contains the six main questions. transcripts/ contains the eight interviews, with follow-up questions.
