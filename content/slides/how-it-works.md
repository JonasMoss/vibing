# How these things actually work

Just enough to drive well.

We skip the computer science. We keep the **three ideas that change what you do**.

---

# It writes what's *likely* — it doesn't look things up

Underneath, it predicts the next stretch of text. At this scale that becomes real
reasoning — but the reflex is always to produce something **plausible**.

That's why it's fast and fluent. It's also why it can be **confidently wrong**.

> **So:** for anything that matters, make it show its source. Verify.

---

# It works on a desk, not in a library

It sees only what's **on the desk right now**: this conversation, the files it has
opened, and its instructions. Nothing else exists to it.

And the desk is a fixed size.

> **So:** one job per conversation. Hand it what it needs — don't bury it.

---

# A cluttered desk gives cluttered answers

Let a chat sprawl for hours and the desk overflows: earlier things slide off,
replies get vague, it "forgets" what you told it.

That's not a bug to fix. It's a desk to clear.

> **So:** when it starts drifting, open a fresh conversation. A clean desk is a
> sharp assistant.

---

# Close it, and the desk is wiped

Next session it starts blank. It does **not** remember your project on its own.

Two ways to make things stick:

- **A file it reads every time** — `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md`: *here's my project,
  here's how I like things.* On the desk automatically, every session.
- **A skill** — a saved routine, for the work you repeat.

> Memory is the AI remembering you. Writing it down is *you* making sure.

---

# So *that's* why we…

| Habit | Because |
|---|---|
| **Plain text** | drops onto the desk cleanly — and you can read the change |
| **`AGENTS.md`** | puts your project on the desk every single time |
| **Skills** | freeze a routine so it runs the same, and you can share it |
| **Fresh sessions** | stop the desk from overflowing |
| **Verify** | it predicts — so it can be certain *and* wrong |

Predict · desk · write-it-down. Every habit in this course falls out of these three.

---
