# Debrief — Strategy and Entrepreneurship, Thursday 22 and Friday 23 October 2026

Written 24 October from Jonas's notes. There was more; add it here as it comes
back. The fixes go forward into Marketing (3 December), where they are a
checklist in `courses/2026-12-marketing/README.md`.

## What worked

- **The referee panel** got the most engagement of anything over the two days.
- **Take a paper apart** went much better than at Finance. It depends on timing,
  though: the agent needs several minutes, and the room waits for it.

## What to change

1. **The terminal.** People struggled with it, got frustrated, and it coloured the
   rest. Next time: the Claude app instead of the terminal.
2. **People sat idle and didn't see the point of the exercises.** Idea for next
   time: their own paper in progress as the thread through the whole course, so
   every exercise is on something they care about. To be evaluated.
3. **Waiting for the agent is dead time.** Start the long jobs (Take a paper apart)
   and do something else while they run.
4. **Literature and DOI checks: lukewarm.** Move them out of the main line, into
   another section.
5. **Say more about sycophancy,** with the evidence (graphs, benchmarks), and build
   the referee panel around it: the referees get a submission-ready package, not
   the source files, in sessions that can't tell it's your paper.
6. **The parking lot didn't work.** We didn't follow through: some parked questions
   were never brought up again. Drop it.
