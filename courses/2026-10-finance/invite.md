# AI in the terminal

**A hands-on session for the Department of Finance**

Friday 16 October 2026, 09.00–16.00, lunch 12.00–13.00\
Room A2 – Blue 1, Campus Oslo\
Taught by Jonas Moss, Department of Data Science and Analytics

## What we will do

The day starts with setup. Everyone gets Claude Code or Codex running on
their own laptop and gets comfortable working with it on their own files.

From there the focus is research. We look at what these tools can do with
your literature, data and writing, and spend a good part of the day on your
own projects. The exact content will adapt to who shows up.

## Who it is for

Everyone in the department. No terminal or programming experience is needed.

## What to bring

- Your own laptop
- A paid Claude or ChatGPT subscription (see below)
- If you can, something real to work on, such as a paper in progress, a
  dataset or a messy project folder

## About the subscription

You need a paid plan from Claude (for Claude Code) or ChatGPT (for Codex).
BI's Gemini subscription will not work, because it cannot access the files on
your computer.

The cheapest paid plans, Claude Pro and ChatGPT Plus, are enough to get
started, but both have usage limits. Claude Pro runs out sooner within a
five-hour window; ChatGPT Plus runs out sooner over a week. On these plans you
cannot use the tool flat out all day, so try it and see what works for you. If
you keep hitting the limit, a higher plan is the fix.
