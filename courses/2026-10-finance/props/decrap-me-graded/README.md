# decrap-me: dummy-graded grading table (practice)

Output from the decrap-me exercise in the Finance run (16 Oct 2026).

**The marks are not real.** The submissions in `decrap-me.zip` are placeholders
with identical text, and the solution guide has no criteria, so nothing could be
assessed. The A–F marks were drawn at random (fixed seed 37911) to practise
filling `grading-table.csv` and the WISEflow grade sheet. All IDs are the
synthetic ones from the zip.

`Comment` marks every row as dummy and flags Groups 1 and 2, whose reports say
"Group (shared template)" instead of their group number.
