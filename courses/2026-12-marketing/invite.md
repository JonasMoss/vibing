# AI in the terminal

**A hands-on session for the Department of Marketing**

Thursday 3 December 2026, 12.00–16.00\
Room to be announced\
Taught by Jonas Moss, Department of Data Science and Analytics

## What we will do

The afternoon starts with setup. Everyone gets Claude Code or Codex running on
their own laptop and gets comfortable working with it on their own files.

From there the focus is research. We look at what these tools can do with your
literature, data and writing. The exact content will adapt to who shows up.

## Who it is for

Everyone in the department. No terminal or programming experience is needed.

## What to bring

- A laptop
- A paid Claude or ChatGPT subscription
- If you can, something real to work on, such as a paper in progress, a
  dataset or a messy project folder
